## Public-release checklist

- LEMON license verified on the canonical INDI portal as PDDL; preserve the visible dataset citation and source link.
- Keep the formal TotalSegmentator v2 citation and CC BY 4.0 link beside the interactive CT explorer.
- Keep the CardioMechanics heart attribution visible and do not use the CC BY-NC asset commercially without permission.
- Confirm that every aggregate AABC or controlled-access result remains figure-only with no participant-level data.
- Preserve all cross-cohort, reference-anatomy, conditional-result, and research-only disclosures.
- Do not replace the current private access policy with shared or public access without an explicit review of this file.
