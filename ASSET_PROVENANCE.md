## Public-release checklist

- MPI-LEMON files in this site trace to the canonical public INDI release, whose page lists PDDL as the data-sharing license; preserve the source link and identify locally generated Atropos/DKT derivatives.
- PTB-XL examples retain the Wagner et al. / PhysioNet v1.0.3 citation, DOI, and CC BY 4.0 notice in this record and in the visible site footer.
- Keep the formal TotalSegmentator v2 citation and CC BY 4.0 link beside the interactive CT explorer.
- Keep the CardioMechanics heart attribution visible and do not use the CC BY-NC asset commercially without permission.
- Confirm that every aggregate AABC or controlled-access result remains figure-only with no participant-level data.
- Preserve all cross-cohort, reference-anatomy, conditional-result, and research-only disclosures.
- Before each public deployment, rerun the build, verify every linked paper/repository URL, and review this record whenever an asset changes.
